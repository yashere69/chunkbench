# Shipping and returns

Indigo Loom is a fictional store used for examples. Everything below is invented.

## Where we ship

We ship to every PIN code in India that our courier partners serve. Before you place an order, enter your PIN code on the product page to see whether delivery is available and how long it will take. International shipping is not available yet.

Orders are dispatched from our studio in Jaipur. Most orders leave the studio within two working days. Hand block printed pieces made to order take seven to ten working days before dispatch, and the product page says so when that applies.

## Delivery times

Metro cities usually receive orders three to five days after dispatch. Other towns take five to eight days. North-east states, Jammu and Kashmir, Ladakh and the Andaman and Nicobar Islands can take up to twelve days. During festival weeks, add two to three days to every estimate.

You will get a WhatsApp message with the tracking link as soon as the courier picks up the parcel. If the tracking page has not updated for four days, write to us and we will chase the courier.

## Shipping charges

Shipping is free on prepaid orders above ₹1,499. Below that we charge ₹79. Cash on delivery is available for orders up to ₹5,000 and carries a fee of ₹49, which is not refunded if the order is returned.

## Returns

You can return unworn, unwashed pieces with tags attached within seven days of delivery. Request a return from the order page or by replying to your order confirmation on WhatsApp. We arrange the pickup; you do not need to ship it yourself.

Some things cannot be returned: dupattas bought on sale, anything altered at your request, and innerwear. Made-to-order pieces can be returned only if they arrive damaged or different from what you ordered.

## Exchanges

We exchange for a different size once per order, free of charge, as long as the size is in stock. If it is not, we refund instead. Exchanges for a different product are treated as a return and a new order.

## Refunds

Prepaid orders are refunded to the original payment method within five working days of the return reaching our studio. Cash on delivery orders are refunded by bank transfer or UPI; we will ask for your details on WhatsApp. We never ask for your card number, PIN or OTP.

## Damaged or wrong items

If something arrives damaged or is not what you ordered, send us a photo within 48 hours of delivery. We will send a replacement at no cost, or refund in full if a replacement is not possible. Please keep the original packaging until the issue is resolved.
